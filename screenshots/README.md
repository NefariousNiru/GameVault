# GameVault screenshots

Capture two **real device** screenshots, then save them here as:

1. `library.png` — your PC or PS5 **THE COLLECTION** screen, with game cards visible.
2. `queue.png` — **Play Queue** with at least two games (avoid exposing your personal Google email or other personal information).

The root README links to these two filenames. Both can be portrait PNGs from your Android phone. Keep the names exactly as shown so the links work automatically after committing the images.
